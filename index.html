About

Detection you can question, not just trust.

I'm a PhD student at the Stratosphere Laboratory (AI Center, FEE CTU in Prague), working where machine learning meets network security: catching malicious behaviour in traffic, and testing whether the explanations behind those detections actually hold up.

I also design environments where autonomous agents learn to attack and defend, and build tools to reveal how those agents behave, not just the score they end up with.

Research directions

Three threads I keep pulling on.

/ explainability

Explanations you can measure

Detecting threats in network traffic, with rigorous metrics for the quality of the explanation behind each decision.

/ autonomy

Autonomous attackers & defenders

Reinforcement-learning environments where offensive and defensive agents co-evolve in realistic scenarios.

/ behaviour

How agents actually behave

Turning raw agent trajectories into interpretable characterisations of strategy: behaviour you can inspect.
