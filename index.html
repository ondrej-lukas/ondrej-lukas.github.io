About

Detection you can question, not just trust.

I'm a PhD student at the Stratosphere Laboratory (AI Center, Faculty of Electrical Engineering, CTU in Prague). My work lives where machine learning meets network security: catching malicious behaviour in traffic, and testing whether the explanations behind those detections actually hold up.

I also design environments where autonomous agents learn to attack and defend, and build tools to reveal how those agents behave — not just the score they end up with.

Research directions

Three threads I keep pulling on.

/ explainability

Explanations you can measure

Detecting threats in network traffic — and building rigorous metrics for the quality of the explanation behind every decision.

/ autonomy

Autonomous attackers & defenders

Reinforcement-learning environments where offensive and defensive agents co-evolve in realistic security scenarios.

/ behaviour

How agents actually behave

Turning raw agent trajectories into interpretable characterisations of strategy — behaviour you can inspect, not just reward.
